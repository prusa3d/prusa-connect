Fleet stats shows how the fleet's prints went, read from the job history. A Statistics page shows five numbers for the last 7 or 30 days, each with its change against the period before. Below them are a heatmap of the busy hours by weekday and the failures of each printer. Each printer's card in the listing gets a line with its success rate, and each printer's Status tab gets a section with its own heatmap row and its recent failures.

It is a browser bundle only. The server checks it against its digest and serves it, and never runs it.

It needs a history. The server keeps no past unless a `history` resource is declared in the configuration, and without one the page says that history is off. The statistics start from the first job the history recorded.

- **Statistics**: a page of its own, with an entry in the sidebar
- **Success rate**: one line on each printer's card in the listing
- **Print statistics**: a section at the foot of each printer's Status tab
- **Fleet figures**: a card a person can place on a dashboard
