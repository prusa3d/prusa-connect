### Added
- [sdk] A driver can describe each reading of a machine, such as the bed temperature, under /readings/<id>/ in the state of the device. `connectorapi.Reading` writes a description, and `connectorapi.ReadReadings` reads the descriptions of one device.
- A Prusa printer describes its bed and, when it has one, its chamber under /readings/ in its state. An XL describes its chamber while its enclosure is on.
- The Moonraker example describes the bed and each heater_generic and temperature_sensor section of a Klipper machine as a reading. The value of each section is written at /temperature/<id>, and the target of a heater at /temperature/<id>_target.

### Changed
- [ui] The temperature chart, the printer card and the printer page show the readings a printer describes, in place of a fixed bed and chamber. The settings of a temperature card offer the readings of the printer it shows.
